# Lab

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QlT5PKUWz6Y - video of my implementation

I chose to create a hologram shader. I added a pulsating effect that changes the power of the Fresnel effect, making the hologram glow stronger and weaker over time. I also used a Lerp node to transition between two color, white and red. This effect could be used as a low health indicator on a character's body. When the player's health is low, the hologram could transiton between red and white to warn the player that they are close to dying.
